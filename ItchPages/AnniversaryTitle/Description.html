Anniversary Title

Sloppie Boppie and its 10th anniversary art replaces the title screen of Hp2, fully animated like the video

Installation

To install, first download the Hp2BaseMod, then download, unzip and place the AnniversaryTitle folder in BepInEx/plugins

Features

  • A Banger
