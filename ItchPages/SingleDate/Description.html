Single Date

Adds HuniePop 1 style single dates

Installation

To install, first download the Hp2BaseMod, then download, unzip and place the SingleDate folder in BepInEx/plugins

Features

  • Hp1 style single dates with every girl, no fucus switching or stamins
  • Toggleable baggage on single dates
  • Customizable max relationship level
  • Derpy animated charms

Important Notes

With the Digital Art Collection DLC installed, charms from the collection will be used. If it's not installed, the charms will use a default image with a "?" on their head. This is not a bug.
