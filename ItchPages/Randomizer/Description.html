Hp2 Randomizer

Randomizes pairings, ailments, favorite affection types and more! Make sure to look at the configs, lots of options.

Installation

To install, first download the Hp2BaseMod, then download, unzip and place the Hp2Randomizer folder in BepInEx/plugins

Features

  • Seeded randomization
  • Toggleable name randomization
  • Toggleable baggage randomizetion
  • Toggleable pairing randomization
  • Toggleable favorite and least favorite affection randomization
  • Toggleable inclusion of Kyu and the Nymphojinn
  • Toggleable focing of special characters to swap with normal characters
  • Toggleable wings for special character swaps
